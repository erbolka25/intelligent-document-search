Working Time and Vacation Policy of Ala-Too Services LLC (fictional company)

1. Working hours
The working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. The working day starts at 9:00 and ends at 18:00, with a lunch break from 13:00 to 14:00. With the manager's approval, an employee may start the working day at any time between 8:00 and 10:00.

2. Remote work
An employee may work from home no more than two days a week. A request for a remote day must be sent to the manager no later than 17:00 on the previous working day. On a remote day, the employee must respond in the work chat from 10:00 to 17:00.

3. Annual vacation
Annual paid vacation is 28 calendar days. Vacation may be split into parts, but one of the parts must be at least 14 calendar days long. A vacation request is submitted to the HR department at least two weeks before the vacation starts.

4. Public holidays
The office is closed on the public holidays of the Kyrgyz Republic. If an employee works on a public holiday at the manager's request, they are given another day off within a month.

5. Sick leave
When ill, an employee informs their manager on the same day by phone or in the work chat. The sick leave certificate must be handed to the HR department within three working days after returning to work.
