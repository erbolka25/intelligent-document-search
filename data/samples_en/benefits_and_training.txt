Employee Benefits and Training at Ala-Too Services LLC (fictional company)

1. Health insurance
After the probation period ends, the employee receives voluntary health insurance. The insurance covers doctor visits and lab tests at partner clinics in Bishkek and Osh. Family members can be added to the insurance at the employee's own expense with a 30 percent discount.

2. Meals
The company reimburses lunches at 3 USD for each day worked. The reimbursement is paid together with the salary.

3. Probation period
The probation period for new employees lasts three months. During the first week, a new employee is assigned a mentor from their department.

4. Training
Each year the company pays for an employee's professional training of up to 450 USD. The course must be approved by the manager before the training starts. After completing the course, the employee gives a copy of the certificate to the HR department.

5. Language classes
Employees can attend English and Kyrgyz language classes free of charge. Classes are held in the office twice a week after 18:00.
