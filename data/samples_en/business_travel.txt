Business Travel Policy of Ala-Too Services LLC (fictional company)

1. Arranging a business trip
A business trip is authorized by an order of the director based on a memo from the employee's manager. Tickets and hotels are booked by the office manager; the employee does not buy them on their own.

2. Daily allowance
The daily allowance for business trips within Kyrgyzstan is 20 USD per day. The daily allowance for business trips abroad is 40 USD per day. The daily allowance is paid before the trip begins.

3. Accommodation
The hotel limit in Bishkek is 50 USD per night. The hotel limit in Osh, Karakol and other cities of Kyrgyzstan is 35 USD per night. Expenses above the limit are paid by the employee unless they were approved by the director in advance.

4. Transportation
Trips between Bishkek and Osh are made by economy-class flight. Trips to Karakol, Naryn and Talas are made by company car or intercity taxi.

5. Trip report
Within three working days after returning, the employee submits an expense report to the accounting department. Original receipts, tickets and the hotel invoice are attached to the report. Approved expenses are reimbursed to the salary card within five working days.
